# Motion pass

Motion thesis: use a stamped check to acknowledge a server-confirmed ticket or cash-out; use small control responses and a moving navigation indicator for continuity. Keep continuously updating prices truthful and keep geometry out of the animation loop.

The user explicitly requested that OS reduced-motion preferences not disable the experience. App-owned motion gates were removed, including the comparison grid's conditional flash durations. This supersedes earlier reports describing OS-based reduced-motion support.

## Implemented

- Ticket check-stamp: one 440ms arrival and a 320ms check stroke, mounted only with an authoritative position or completed cash-out. It can also play when a restored ticket mounts; it is not a new-trade counter.
- Controls: 120ms press response; the active mobile navigation surface moves in 220ms and can be interrupted by the next selection.
- Company chart: a short opacity transition when the keyed company chart mounts. Streaming price updates do not redraw a fictional interpolated price path or restart the entrance.
- Value bar: full-size layers scale horizontally; the paid marker translates inside an SVG. No animated widths or changing marker positions drive HTML layout.
- Help: 220ms opening treatment with immediate native Escape/close and focus restoration.
- Scenario: directional disclosure indicator and short content opacity transition.
- Pending feedback stops after three cycles; the plot-twist pulse stops after two. Status text remains visible afterward.

## Verification

Chromium, emulated mobile 390×844 and desktop 1280×720:

- Rapid Market → News → Ticket → Market changes ended in the expected view, with a transform-only indicator transition and no horizontal overflow.
- A fictional buy produced the server's accepted status and a single 0.44s ticket-stamp animation. Cash-out reached the confirmed exit heading.
- Both value layers retained a 275px layout width while their visual transforms changed. An initial marker overflow was corrected with an SVG track; mobile client/scroll widths then matched at 375px.
- Help opened with its animation; Escape closed it immediately and returned focus to How to play.
- Desktop ticket actions and confirmation remained visible in the short viewport.
- TypeScript and ESLint passed. Production build succeeded with the pre-existing large comparison chunk warning. The changed-target design scan returned no findings.

No performance trace, physical-touch test, or Safari verification was performed. Transform usage and geometry checks do not constitute a measured frame-rate or zero-reflow claim. No motion dependency was added.
