# Ticket P identity

Ticket P adapts the original Ticket S vector study selected from `/scratch/brand.html` for Pupside. It preserves the yellow ticket silhouette, dark monogram, and stroke weight. The original `ticket-s.svg` remains as a historical design asset.

Bricolage Grotesque Latin variable font: `@fontsource-variable/bricolage-grotesque@5.3.0/files/bricolage-grotesque-latin-wght-normal.woff2`, distributed under the included SIL Open Font License. Copied from the reviewed scratch font assets. Used at weight 800 for the app wordmark.
