# RoboPup pose sheet

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the mascot scratch studies, then approved for live gameplay. Identity reference: `../landing-v2/trading-desk.jpg`. The generated PNG is retained unchanged, with transparency; the component displays one of its three square cells.

Prompt: Create one transparent 3:1 sprite sheet with three equal square cells. Preserve the reference's polished purple 3D robot puppy, black faceplate, warm yellow happy arc eyes, cream muzzle and circular joints, antennae with gold round tips. Cell 1: puppy with a yellow rubber stamp and cream ticket. Cell 2: seated puppy with a paw on a small yellow service bell. Cell 3: puppy holding a cream receipt with five purple rules. No readable text, borders, confetti, floor or backdrop. Keep each pose inside its cell with clear silhouettes for small UI use.

The scratch animations move the full pose and supporting UI. They are composition/motion studies, not articulated limb animation.
